## Implemented

- 031: `cutRanges` validates all input ranges, unions overlapping/adjacent ranges,
  and removes the merged ranges from last to first. `closeGap` finds the gap
  around the requested time and refuses a global closure that would cut content
  on another cut-mode track, including a locked one. Follow/off behavior and
  marker mapping reuse the existing ripple removal path.
- 032: `insertTime` and `insertClip` in insert mode. Insertions snap within half
  a frame of a cut, using the reference's sample-rounded half-frame reach. Short
  residual pieces produce a warning. Cut tracks split; later follow clips shift
  while spanning follow clips stay whole; off tracks stay in place. Locked
  tracks produce a warning. Insertion keeps markers exactly at the insertion
  point in place and moves later markers without changing their durations.
- 033: ripple `trim` shifts following clips on each edited track once, then
  propagates the time change to other tracks and markers. Ripple head trimming
  keeps the clip's timeline start while changing source-in/duration. Linked
  partners share the trim delta. Both growing and shrinking edges are supported.
- 034: `roll` adjusts a meeting cut and both source ranges without changing
  their outer timeline bounds. Matching linked cuts on other tracks roll too.
  Missing neighbors, non-meeting clips, consumed clips, locked partners, and
  insufficient source handles reject atomically.
- 035: `slip` changes source-in directly without changing placement/duration.
  It includes linked partners by default and supports `includeLinked: false`.
  Its delta is source time, including at non-unit speed.
- 036: `slide` moves a clip and its linked partners while trimming touching
  previous/next clips. It keeps the selected clip's source range and duration.
  With both neighbors, the sequence end stays fixed. Negative starts, consumed
  neighbors, overlaps, insufficient source handles, and locked partners reject.

New edits use the same schemas, coordinator, revision checks, save queue, and
undo/redo as earlier work. Sub-millisecond overlap slivers are closed after the
timeline command and before validation, preserving source content. Link groups
are normalized after the operation.

The timeline's Time edits dialog exposes insert time, gap closure, and multiple
range cuts. Gap closure uses the selected clip's track, or the first unlocked
targeted track. The inspector exposes numeric slip, slide, roll-next-cut, and
ripple edge changes. Bin sources have Insert at playhead, and Shift-drop inserts.
Pointer alternatives are Alt-drag to slip, Alt+Shift-drag to slide, Alt-drag an
edge to roll, and Shift-drag an edge to ripple trim. These modifier choices are
SideCut controls; complete reference keymap/pointer parity is a later phase.

The session context now retains its identity across Vite hot refreshes. Testing
encountered a transient provider/hook context mismatch during module replacement;
the change prevents those modules from creating different contexts. Production
does not retain any Vite hot state.

blue, and yellow frames and a 48 kHz 440 Hz tone. The imported fixture has three
linked picture/sound pairs at timeline 0, 2, and 4 seconds, each two seconds
long, with source-in 1, 3, and 5 seconds. No private media, microphone, camera,
upload, or cloud processing was used.
